Information This trophy

Name: Flixie Age: 7 sparks old (that’s about 14 in flame years) Height: Just tall enough to toast a marshmallow Hometown: Emberglow Hollow, deep inside the Hearthwood Forest

Bio: Flixie was born from the very first spark of a cozy campfire under a full moon. With glowing cheeks, a fiery swirl of hair, and a giggle that crackles like dry twigs, she’s one of the rare Whimflames — magical fire spirits that bring warmth, joy, and a little chaos wherever they go. She spends her days lighting lanterns for lost forest creatures, dancing on fallen leaves, and sometimes accidentally setting toast on fire (oops!). Though small, Flixie dreams of one day becoming a Great Flame Guardian who watches over the balance of warmth and wildness in the world.
