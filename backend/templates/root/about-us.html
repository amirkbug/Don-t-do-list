About NONETOAST Studio

At NONETOAST, everything starts with a love for details:From UI/UX design and 2D/3D animations to custom websites built with modern technologies like HTML, CSS, JavaScript,react,next.js and backend development using frameworks such as Django.We also specialize in game development, concept art, and crafting imaginative interactive experiences.

About the Don’t Do List Project

Don’t Do List is a unique and personal project by NONETOAST — originally a front-end challenge with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JS. But it grew into something more meaningful: an emotional and mindful experience. It’s not your typical to-do list. Its poetic, visual design invites users to reflect — not just on what to do, but also what not to do.

With both “To-Do” and “Don’t Do” sections, it encourages intentional productivity. Soon, it’ll evolve with Django, adding user accounts, MVT architecture, and a trophy system — turning tasks into something playful and rewarding.
